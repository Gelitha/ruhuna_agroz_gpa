# Product Roadmap 🗺️

This document outlines the planned future enhancements and strategic direction for the **University of Ruhuna — Faculty of Agriculture GPA Calculator & Academic Advisor Engine**.

---

## 🎯 Short-Term Goals (v2.2.0 - v2.3.0)

- [ ] **PWA (Progressive Web App) Support:**
  - Add `manifest.json` and service worker caching for full offline installation on Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS ("Install App" prompt).
- [ ] **Advanced PDF Transcript Designer:**
  - Client-side PDF generation using `pdfmake` or `html2pdf.js` with official Faculty of Agriculture header formatting, watermarks, and verification timestamps.
- [ ] **Quick Grade Presets & Bulk Fill:**
  - One-click grade fill templates (e.g., "Fill all remaining with B+", "Clear unreleased grades") per semester.
- [ ] **Keyboard Navigation & Accessibility (a11y):**
  - Arrow key navigation between course rows and rapid letter grade typing (`A`, `B`, `C` directly triggering dropdown selections).
- [ ] **Custom Course Tags & Notes:**
  - Allow students to attach short private notes (e.g., "Assignment pending", "Exam date: Dec 12") to individual courses.

---

## 🚀 Medium-Term Goals (v3.0.0)

- [ ] **Direct MIS / PDF Result Sheet OCR / Parser:**
  - Drag-and-drop official PDF result notifications to automatically parse and populate semester grades without manual copy-pasting.
- [ ] **Faculty Elective Recommendation Engine:**
  - Suggest elective courses based on the student's intended Year 4 specialization department (e.g. recommending advanced Crop Physiology or Entomology for Crop Science majors).
- [ ] **Visual Semester-by-Semester Grade Distribution Chart:**
  - Bar / donut charts showing the percentage of A's, B's, and C's earned throughout the student's academic journey.
- [ ] **Multi-Language Interface (English / Sinhala / Tamil):**
  - Add bilingual / trilingual UI localized for Sri Lankan university undergraduates.
- [ ] **Target Grade Optimization Solver:**
  - An optimization algorithm that suggests the easiest combination of grades across remaining courses (taking credit weights into account) to reach a desired target CGPA.

---

## 🔮 Long-Term Vision (v4.0.0+)

- [ ] **Encrypted Cloud Sync (Optional & Privacy-Preserving):**
  - Zero-knowledge end-to-end encrypted backup (e.g. via personal Google Drive or decentralized private key) so students can sync profiles across phone and laptop without centralized servers.
- [ ] **Faculty Examination Regulations Alert System:**
  - Automated detection of critical academic risk rules (e.g., warning if more than the allowable number of D/E grades are accumulated, or if probation criteria are triggered).
- [ ] **Alumni / Postgraduate Academic Portfolio Generator:**
  - Export standardized academic performance summaries formatted for scholarship and postgraduate degree applications (e.g., Commonwealth, Erasmus+, Fulbright).
