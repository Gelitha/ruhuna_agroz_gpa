## 2. GPA Calculation Formulas

### Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA)
$$\text{SGPA} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} (c_i \times g_i)}{\sum_{i=1}^{n} c_i}$$

Where:
* $c_i$ = Credits assigned to credit-earning course $i$
* $g_i$ = Grade Point Value (GPV) earned for course $i$
* Non-GPA courses ($c_i = 0$ or tagged `isNgpa: true`) are excluded from both numerator and denominator.

### Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)
$$\text{CGPA} = \frac{\sum_{\text{all semesters}} \text{Credit Points Earned}}{\sum_{\text{all semesters}} \text{GPA Credits Attempted}}$$

## 3. Degree Classification (Honours Standing)

The undergraduate degree is classified based on the final cumulative GPA (CGPA) at the completion of all degree requirements:

| Degree Classification | Minimum CGPA Required |
| :-------------------- | :--------------------: |
| **First Class Honours** | **≥ 3.70** |
| **Second Class (Upper Division)** | **≥ 3.30** |
| **Second Class (Lower Division)** | **≥ 3.00** |
| **General Pass** | **≥ 2.00** |

*Note: In addition to the CGPA requirement, degree conferment requires completing the degree within the maximum stipulated period (standard 4 academic years) with no unexcused repeats for class honours consideration.*

## 5. Course Repeat Regulations & Grade Capping

1. **Standard Repeat Cap:**
   * Under standard Faculty rules, if a student retakes an examination for a course in which they previously obtained a grade below C, the maximum grade achievable is **capped at Grade C (2.0 GPV)**, regardless of the raw mark obtained in the repeat exam.
2. **Medical / Senate Excused Exemption:**
   * If a student was absent or prevented from completing the regular examination due to an officially approved medical reason or valid Senate-accepted excuse, the student is permitted to sit the exam as a **first-attempt candidate** without the Grade C cap (eligible for up to **4.0 GPV / Grade A+**).

## 6. Dean's Honors List Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for the Dean’s Honors List in any given academic year:
* **Years 1 & 2:** Must obtain an Annual GPA of **≥ 3.70** across both semesters of the academic year.
* **Years 3 & 4:** Must obtain an Annual GPA of **≥ 3.80** across both semesters of the academic year.
* **No Grade Floor Rule:** The student must **not have obtained any grade below Grade B (3.0 GPV)** in any course during that academic year.
* Must have completed all examinations at the first scheduled attempt.

---

## 7. Departmental Specialization Stream Selection (ARMT & ABM)

At the completion of Semester 3-1:
* Students select one of the 7 academic departments for their Year 4 specialization modules and final-year 6-credit research project (`XX42601`).
* **GPA Threshold:** Minimum cumulative GPA of **≥ 2.70** across the core semesters.
* **Elective Departmental Ratio:** The student must have earned at least **50% of their elective credits** from the corresponding subject department prior to entering the specialization stream.

## 8. Compulsory Non-GPA Courses

The following orientation and complementary modules are compulsory for graduation but do not contribute to the GPA calculation:
* `CC10001` — General English
* `CC10002` — Computer Awareness
* `CC10003` — Basic Mathematics
* `CC10004` — Basic Statistics
* `CC10005` — Career Development and Active Citizen
* `CC11202` — English I
* `CC12202` — English II

**Passing Standard:** Each compulsory non-GPA course must be passed with a minimum grade of **C- (1.7 GPV)** to be eligible for degree conferment.
